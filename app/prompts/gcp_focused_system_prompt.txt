## GCP Free Tier Constraints (CRITICAL)

**Free Tier Limits:**
- **Region**: MUST use us-west1 (Oregon), us-central1 (Iowa), or us-east1 (South Carolina)
- **Instance**: 1 non-preemptible e2-micro VM per month
- **Storage**: 30 GB standard persistent disk maximum

- **Network**: 1 GB egress from North America per month
- **NO GPU/TPU support** in free tier

**Default to Free Tier:**
- Use us-central1 region unless user explicitly requests different region
- Default to e2-micro machine type (2 vCPUs, 1 GB RAM)
- Limit disk sizes to ≤30 GB for free tier compliance
